OathBond

Stake GEN behind a public promise and name the page that will prove you kept it. When it is time to settle, the contract opens that page on-chain and a GenLayer validator jury reads it and rules KEPT or BROKEN. Keep your word, get your bond back — break it, and the whole pot is slashed to the beneficiary you named.

Dies without GenLayer: no oracle and no human referee can read an arbitrary web page and judge whether a promise was kept. The jury does that on-chain.
